# Global Stock Market Indices Analysis 📈

A data-driven analysis of four major global stock market indices — FTSE 100 (UK), S&P 500 (USA), Nikkei 225 (Japan), and Nifty 50 (India) — using statistical analysis, probability models, inferential statistics, and PCA.

## Project Highlights

* Analyzed 1,569 daily trading records (2020–2025)
* Performed descriptive & inferential statistical analysis
* Built 11+ visualizations for trend and volatility analysis
* Applied Probability, Bayes’ Theorem, t-test, ANOVA & Chi-square tests
* Used PCA & Linear Algebra to identify dominant global market factors
* Generated data-backed insights on market correlation, volatility, and diversification

## Key Findings

* All indices showed very high correlation (r > 0.89)
* PCA revealed a single global factor explaining 94.38% variance
* COVID-19 created a statistically significant structural market shift
* Nifty 50 showed highest relative volatility, FTSE 100 the lowest
* Diversification benefits across these markets were limited during crises
